# Walkthrough script (for the handoff video)

About eight minutes, for a founder or marketing lead. Record against the Online Retail II demo
(`just seed retail`) at 1366 × 900.

1. **Sign in** (0:00). Access is by invitation; you get an email link.
2. **Summary** (0:30). Read the top sentence: it says which customers the numbers cover. Click an
   underlined clause (the date range, a channel) to change it; the link in the address bar now
   reproduces this exact view, so it can be shared. Read the three findings aloud: each links to
   the page that proves it.
3. **Vitals** (1:30). Each row compares the latest complete period with the one before.
   "Repeat within 90 days" only counts customers who have had 90 days, so it never punishes new
   customers for being new. Hover any underlined measure for its definition.
4. **Cohorts** (2:30). Rows are the months customers first bought; columns are months since. The
   grey staircase on the right is the future, not zero. Darker means more customers came back.
   Switch to "Cumulative repeat" to compare cohorts on one rising curve. Click a cohort to see
   its curve against the median of the last six; pin up to three to compare.
5. **Repeat** (4:00). Half of returning customers come back within N days; this is the window for
   a follow-up email. Below, which first purchase brings people back, with its range: a narrow
   range is reliable, a wide one is a hunch.
6. **Segments and Customers** (5:00). Click "At risk" to see who they are. Open one customer: their
   orders on a timeline, whether they have passed the store's own lapse threshold, and the
   chance they are still a customer.
7. **Channels** (6:00). Without spend data the page says how to add it. With spend: cost to
   acquire, 365-day value, value-to-cost ratio and the month each channel pays back.
8. **Data** (7:00). When the numbers were refreshed, whether every check passed, and how they
   reconcile with the source. If a refresh ever fails, yesterday's numbers stay and this page
   says so.
